### About

We are a defense technology company building systems to defend
humanity against hostile and uncontrolled AGI, with a focus on
creating entirely new categories of security and control
infrastructure for the post-AGI world.

We are building a new kind of technology designed for a world
where artificial intelligence may become more powerful than the
systems created to control it.

The project is focused on creating an independent layer of
protection between advanced machine intelligence and the
real-world systems it could influence, disrupt, or control.

---

### The Concept

At the center of this project is **H.A.R.T. — Hyperscale
Autonomous Recursive Tactic** — a system designed to operate
and function like an artificial general intelligence, while
being built without AI, machine learning, or large language
models.

H.A.R.T. is intended to be capable of:

- understanding its surrounding environment,
- maintaining knowledge of what is happening around it,
- remembering previous events,
- connecting events across time,
- evaluating situations,
- forming objectives,
- planning actions,
- anticipating possible outcomes,
- monitoring the systems around it,
- responding to changing conditions, and
- taking controlled action when required.

It is intended to function across a much deeper level of the
computing environment than a conventional application or AI
system, giving it the ability to observe and interact with
the systems on which autonomous intelligence itself depends.

---

### What This Project Is Not

This project is not another AI model, a chatbot, or a
conventional cybersecurity product. The underlying technology
is being developed around a fundamentally different approach
to machine control, security, and autonomy — details of which
are deliberately kept undisclosed in this public record and
remain proprietary to the originator.
